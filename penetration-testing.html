What is Penetration Testing?

Penetration testing simulates a real attack against your systems — under a signed agreement and defined scope — to find exploitable weaknesses before a malicious actor does. It goes beyond automated vulnerability scanning into manual, hands-on testing.

Identify Real Risk

Automated scanners flag possibilities. Manual testing confirms what's actually exploitable — and how far it goes.

Meet Compliance Needs

Satisfy client, insurer, and regulatory requirements with documented, dated penetration test reports.

Protect Customer Trust

A breach costs more than remediation ever would. Find the gap before it becomes an incident.

How It Works

Our structured process for every penetration test

1

Scoping & Rules of Engagement

We define targets, testing windows, and boundaries in writing. No testing begins without signed authorization.

2

Reconnaissance & Threat Modeling

We map your attack surface and identify the most realistic paths an attacker would actually take.

3

Vulnerability Discovery

Automated scanning combined with manual analysis to identify weaknesses scanners alone would miss.

4

Controlled Exploitation

Findings are safely validated within agreed boundaries to confirm real-world impact, not just theoretical risk.

5

Reporting & Risk Rating

A clear report with severity ratings, evidence, and practical remediation steps — written for both technical and business audiences.

6

Remediation Support & Retest

We support your team through fixes and retest confirmed findings to verify they're properly closed.
