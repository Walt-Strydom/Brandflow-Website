Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What to expect from a Microsoft 365, Azure, or Dynamics 365 engagement.

These engagements are scoped per project. Cost depends on factors like user/tenant count, environment complexity, data volume, and how much customisation or integration is required. We'll assess your requirements on a discovery call and provide a tailored quote — there's no fixed package pricing for these services.

Timelines vary widely by scope — a straightforward Microsoft 365 mailbox migration can take days, while a full Dynamics 365 CRM/ERP implementation with data migration and custom workflows can take several weeks to months. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we understand your requirements.

No — Microsoft 365, Azure, and Dynamics 365 licensing is billed directly by Microsoft (or your reseller) based on your usage and tier. Our quote covers the implementation, migration, and configuration work, not the underlying subscription costs.

We follow Microsoft's recommended migration paths, validate data before and after cutover, and configure security baselines (MFA, Conditional Access, role-based access) as part of every engagement — not as an afterthought.

Every engagement includes a hypercare period immediately after go-live to resolve issues quickly. After that, we're available for ad-hoc support and ongoing optimisation reviews — there's no mandatory long-term contract.
