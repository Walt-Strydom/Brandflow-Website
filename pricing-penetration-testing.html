Our Process

How We Price

We believe in transparent, fair pricing. Here's how we arrive at a number.

1. Scoping Call

We start by understanding what you want tested — target systems, environments, testing windows, and any compliance drivers.

2. Rules of Engagement

We define scope and boundaries in writing and provide a proposal with a fixed price and testing timeline for sign-off.

3. Test & Report

We test within the agreed scope, deliver a full report with remediation guidance, and retest confirmed fixes at no extra charge.

Scope Drivers

What Shapes the Price

These are the key factors we consider when scoping a penetration testing engagement.

Attack Surface Size

The number of hosts, endpoints, applications, or API routes in scope directly drives testing time and depth.

Testing Depth

A focused vulnerability assessment differs significantly from a full manual penetration test with exploitation.

Application Complexity

User roles, authentication flows, and business logic complexity all affect how long thorough testing takes.

Testing Approach

Black-box, grey-box, or white-box testing require different levels of access and reconnaissance effort.

Compliance Requirements

Regulated industries requiring specific report formats, audit evidence, or annual testing cadences add scope.
